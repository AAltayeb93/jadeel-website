Sponsorship isn't a donation — it's an investment decision for the sponsor. Pitching before readiness is complete weakens your odds and spends a relationship you may not get a second shot at.

## Five readiness elements

1. **A clear value proposition**: what exactly does the sponsor gain — visibility, impact, reach to a segment?
2. **Measurable impact indicators**: what will you prove to the sponsor once the sponsorship ends?
3. **Tiered packages**: options that fit different support sizes, not a single number.
4. **A disciplined narrative**: a story that links need to solution to impact without exaggeration.
5. **Operational readiness**: a real ability to execute, document and report.

## Test your initiative

If two or more of these are missing, the initiative likely needs **Support & Sponsorship Readiness** before approaching anyone — followed by building the **Smart Sponsor Package**.

The goal isn't to dress up the pitch, but to give the sponsor a clear decision they can defend inside their own organization.
